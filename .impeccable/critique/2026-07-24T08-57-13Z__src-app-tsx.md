## Overall Impression

This is a disciplined, genuinely authored implementation of its own design law — the restraint is executed, not just documented, and the engineering under it (hand-rolled /work/:slug history, complete reduced-motion coverage) is careful. The single biggest opportunity is the gap between "case studies are shareable deep links" as product law and what a crawler actually sees: every shared link previews as the generic homepage card.

## What's Working

1. **Reduced-motion coverage is complete, not token.** Component-level `useReducedMotion()` branches in Hero, Plate, CaseStudy plus CSS-level kills (raking-light off, Develop filter off, view-transitions collapsed to 180ms).
2. **Deep-link/history engineering without a router.** pushState/replaceState/popstate, unknown-slug fallback, and `initialCaseStudyId()` so direct loads open the spread without a spurious morph.
3. **The emotional close.** "One email is enough" → Monument "Let's work together." → "Replies within one business day." Specific, real reassurance at the highest-stakes moment.

## Priority Issues

- **[P1] No per-route social meta for /work/:slug.** Static og tags in index.html + client-only SPA means every shared case-study link previews as the generic homepage card. This breaks PRODUCT.md's own "shareable deep links" constraint at the first impression — before the prospect even taps. **Fix:** build-time prerender/meta-injection per slug keyed off content.json. **Suggested command:** /impeccable harden
- **[P1] Hero DOM order inverts reading order for AT.** Hero.tsx writes the index nav before the Monument name and relies on CSS `order` for visual order; tab/screen-reader order announces a 3-item nav list before "Nils Vogelaar" (WCAG 1.3.2). **Fix:** reorder the JSX itself, monument markup first. **Suggested command:** /impeccable harden
- **[P2] CaseStudy loses its own heading discipline.** "Overview" gets `<h2 class="t-label">`; Highlights and Gallery get no heading. Screen-reader users can't jump to the spec sheet; skimmers lose the framing. **Fix:** matching t-label h2s for Highlights (and Gallery). **Suggested command:** /impeccable polish
- **[P2] Primary CTA ignores availability state.** Contact.tsx hardcodes "Email me"; only the sentence below changes across available/limited/unavailable. Flipping the CMS to unavailable makes the loudest element on the page contradict the copy under it. **Fix:** per-state CTA copy. **Suggested command:** /impeccable harden
- **[P3] "Colophon" has no accessible synonym and there's no skip link.** `aria-label="Contact"` on the nav link preserves the editorial voice for sighted users while giving AT/search a plain word; add a skip-to-content link before the fixed header. **Suggested command:** /impeccable clarify

## Persona Red Flags

**Jordan (first-timer, skimming between calls):** the only path to contact is the word "Colophon"; no CTA above the fold — she must scroll Hero → 3 plates → Essay → Specifications to find the one action the site exists for.

**Sam (screen reader / keyboard):** hero announces the index list before the name (P1); no skip-to-content link, so every load means tabbing the full fixed header; "Let's work together." is a plain `<p>`, invisible to heading navigation; Highlights/Gallery have no heading landmark.

**Casey (mobile, via shared /work/:slug):** the share preview she saw was the generic homepage card, not the project (P1). Once on the page the experience is solid: overlay opens clean, controls thumb-reachable, CTA duplicated at body end.
