# Surface brief: portfolio one-pager (public site)

**Scope:** the full public one-page site (hero / oplossingen / werk / werkwijze / about / contact) plus the case-study spread at /work/:slug.
**Visitor mode:** Persuade. Since the October 2026 positioning (`.agents/product-marketing.md`) the reader is the warm lead *and* cold inflow from LinkedIn/outreach; a first-time visitor must grasp the offer, believe it, and see the next step within seconds. The work still leads as proof.

**Audience & job:** webshop owners (€0,5–20 mln), mkb operations managers, agency owners (white-label), founders. Job: "zet AI werkend in mijn proces" and "bouw wat wij zelf niet gebouwd krijgen".

**Action:** one primary action, a kennismakingsgesprek (mailto with subject today; `contact.bookingUrl` takes over when a calendar link exists). Reachable from the hero on every width, the nav, the case pages, and the green close.

**Proof/content:** Short Commerce Weekly Report (lead werkstuk, shown as a typeset pipeline diagram — closed client portal, no client data), FleetDisk, Shaftlab, BerijdersApp, then the sites group compacted. Every figure is a scope-fact from the product-marketing doc; no testimonials.

**Constraints:** Signal world and DESIGN.md unchanged in grammar; content.json stays the only content source; NL first; WCAG 2.2 AA; reduced motion complete; /work/:slug deep links and prerendered meta preserved.

**Unresolved:** booking link; whether to publish the 12–20 h/week capacity figure; customer quotes need name + permission before use.

## Direction contract

THESIS: the page sells one thing — handwerk tussen systemen eruit — and proves each claim with shipped work one click away. It refuses the service-list portfolio ("React, Vue, TypeScript…") it replaces.

OWN-WORLD: Signal, inherited whole: Geist caps at lh .82, Sometype Mono in [ brackets ], 4px chrome, black tiles, hairlines; dark → off-white → dark → off-white → green section themes.

STORY: a visitor reads the offer, recognises their own sentence ("We tikken alles over."), follows it to the werkstuk that proves the fix, checks price/data/capacity guarantees, meets Nils, books a kennismaking.

FIRST VIEWPORT: dither field full-bleed; [ NILS VOGELAAR ] eyebrow; 12-column display promise "Ik koppel je systemen en zet AI aan het werk." bottom-left at ~100px/1440; below it the practice line and the solid [ Plan een kennismaking ↗ ] CTA on every width, no systems row.

FORM: extension of the established Signal surface; owner-pinned lamalama grammar, no concept roll (brief-pinned beats the roll); code-led.

SIGNATURE: the oplossing row — customer quote in display type, the fix, and a bracketed proof link that opens the case spread in place.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
