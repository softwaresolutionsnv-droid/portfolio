# Design System: Nils Vogelaar — Portfolio

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Signal"**

The portfolio is a literal translation of lamalama.com's grammar (pinned by the owner, 6 Sep 2026) into a solo practice's proof-site: bold grotesk caps at line-height .82, a mono UI voice typeset in `[ brackets ]` and `( parens )`, a full-bleed dither field as the hero's one material, a floating nav with a per-section tagline, a right-hand card stack, work as a ruled list with image strips, and themes owned entirely by the section in view. It replaces "Concrete Monograph" (Archivo Expanded + serif, oxide accent, radius 0) outright — nothing from that world survives, and it is now an anti-reference, not a fallback.

Scrolling is Lenis-smoothed (`lerp: 0.1`) and drives every section boundary: `watchSections()` (`src/lib/motion.ts`) flips `data-theme` on `<html>` the moment a section crosses 50% of the viewport, in either scroll direction. There is no visitor-facing toggle — the theme is a property of what is currently on screen, not a preference.

The page runs hero (dark) → oplossingen (light) → work (dark) → werkwijze (light) → about (light) → contact (green). Three moments carry the display type at full scale (the hero headline above all, the Oplossingen/Werkwijze section titles at Headline size, the Contact title); everywhere else — nav, buttons, chips, meta, captions — speaks in Sometype Mono. Color is just as rationed: one dark ground, one off-white ground, one green field, and a single red accent confined to two literal marks.

**Key Characteristics:**
- One dark ground, one off-white ground, one green field — section-owned, never chosen by the visitor
- Geist bold caps at line-height 0.82 for the display moments (hero outranks every section title); Sometype Mono is the UI voice everywhere else
- 4px radius on primary chrome (nav, cards, buttons, tiles), stepping to 3px on avatars and 2px on chips; nothing else rounds
- Signal red is reserved to exactly two marks: the N-mark's third bar and the live-clock dot
- A glyph-dither shader is the hero's one WebGL surface and stays there
- Every text entrance is a GSAP word/line mask; every motion effect disables under `prefers-reduced-motion`

## Colors

A duotone chrome lit by one signal accent and one section-owned field; two further hues are wired into the theme system but not yet assigned to any section.

### Primary
- **Signal Red** (`rgb(230 57 70)`): the system's one true accent. Used in exactly two places in the whole codebase — the N-mark's third bar (`Mark.tsx`) and the live-clock dot's blink (`BottomBar.tsx`).

### Secondary
- **Signal Green** (`rgb(208 255 126)`): the Contact section's field, entered only via `[data-theme="green"]` — the page's one non-monochrome ground, and its closing note.

### Neutral
- **Dark** (`rgb(26 28 28)`): the hero and work ground; also the ink used on the off-white and green sections.
- **Black** (`rgb(0 0 0)`): the fixed background of chrome that ignores section theme entirely — chips, the contact card, spec tiles, the menu overlay.
- **White** (`rgb(255 255 255)`): the elevated tone on light/green sections (`--bg2`), mirroring Black's role on dark.
- **Off-Black** (`rgb(12 12 12)`): the ink on light/green sections.
- **Off-White** (`rgb(249 244 235)`): the oplossingen, werkwijze and about ground; also the ink used on the dark section.

### Reserved (unused)
- **Pink** (`rgb(255 198 218)`) and **Blue** (`rgb(220 242 244)`): a fourth `data-theme="pink"` ground and a blue swatch are fully defined in `index.css` (chip, navbar and side-card selectors already branch on `pink`), but `App.tsx`'s section list only ever assigns `dark`, `light`, or `green`. Both are inert until a section claims them.

### Named Rules
**The Section-Owned Theme Rule.** `data-theme` is set only by `watchSections()`'s 50%-viewport trigger — never by a stored preference or a visible control.
**The One Signal Rule.** Red appears nowhere but the N-mark and the clock dot. It is not a hover, link, or focus color.
**The Fixed-Black Rule.** Chips, the contact card, spec tiles, and the menu overlay hold a black/off-white pairing at all times — they read against `rgb(0 0 0)` regardless of the live section theme, not against the theme's own `--bg2`.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Geist (self-hosted via `@fontsource-variable/geist`, family "Geist Variable", stack `"Geist Variable", "Geist", "Helvetica Neue", Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`; the live scale only calls on 400 and 700).
**UI / Label Font:** Sometype Mono (self-hosted via `@fontsource/sometype-mono`, 400 and 500; both fonts are imported in `src/main.tsx`, Google Fonts and its preconnects are gone, and there is deliberately no font preload: a woff2 preload competed with the JS bundle and slowed FCP) — every piece of interface voice: nav, labels, chips, buttons, meta bar, captions.

**Character:** a grotesk display set tight enough to look poured, against a mono voice that never exceeds 0.75rem — one confident headline moment surrounded by a quiet, technical register.

### Hierarchy
- **Display Hero** (700, `clamp(2.6rem, 0.25rem + 6.6vw, 7.25rem)`, lh 0.82, uppercase, `text-wrap: balance`): `.t-display.t-display--hero` — the hero headline only. It runs the full 12 columns as two authored lines and outranks every section title (6 Oct 2026 critique: it had been smaller than the Services title). Its 7.25rem endpoint is off the old ramp on purpose; it is a recorded ramp step, not drift.
- **Display** (700, `clamp(2.6rem, 0.5rem + 4.2vw, 7.2rem)`, lh 0.82, uppercase, tracking -0.02em): `.t-display` — the Contact section title (balanced over 7 columns) and the base of the hero setting.
- **Headline** (700, `clamp(3rem, 1.125rem + 3.75vw, 6.75rem)`, lh 0.82, uppercase): `.t-display-sm` — the Oplossingen and Werkwijze titles, the case-study title, and the menu overlay's link list.
- **Title** (400, `clamp(1.5rem, 0.875rem + 1.25vw, 2.66rem)`, lh 1.05): `.t-h4` — work-row titles, the Oplossingen intro and the case-study lede.
- **Body Large** (400, `clamp(1.25rem, 0.9375rem + 0.63vw, 1.8rem)`, lh 1.2): the hero practice line, the Contact pitch, the Werkwijze kop (balanced, 30ch).
- **Body** (400, `clamp(0.9375rem, 0.85rem + 0.2vw, 1.14rem)`, lh 1.4): running copy — Werkwijze text (at 0.75 opacity), credit values, spec-tile text.
- **Label** (400, 0.6875rem, lh 1.6, uppercase): `.t-mono` — the site's UI voice: nav tagline, buttons, chips, meta bar, every `Mono` component label.

Two intermediate display sizes exist outside the frontmatter roles: `.t-h2` (`clamp(2rem, 0.75rem + 2.5vw, 4.5rem)`, sentence case) for the Work section's intro line, and `.t-h3` (`clamp(1.75rem, 0.8125rem + 1.88vw, 3.5rem)`) for the About section's drop-indented intro paragraph and the Oplossing row's visitor sentence (second person, 16ch, balanced). `.t-body-md` carries the Oplossing aanpak. Two smaller mono steps, `.t-mono-sm` (0.6875rem, weight 500, lh 1.8) and `.t-mono-md` (0.75rem), carry chip and spec-tile captions; `.t-mono-sm` differs from `.t-mono` by weight and line-height, not size, because the mono voice has an 11px floor (`.chip` shares it). `.t-code` (0.8125rem monospace) is still live: SpecTile values use it.

### Named Rules
**The Bracket Rule.** Mono labels are typeset, not bordered: `.bracket` wraps content in a literal `[ ` … ` ]`, `.paren` in `( ` … ` )` via CSS `content`. Arrows (`↗ ↖ ← →`) are typeset characters, never icon glyphs.
**The Tight-Cap Rule.** Every uppercase display setting (Display, Headline) sits at line-height 0.82 with -0.02em tracking; that combination never appears at body or label sizes.

## Layout

A 12-column grid (`.grid-12`, column gap `var(--gap)`) inside a fluid-margin container (`.container`, side padding `max(1rem, 40/1440 · 100vw)`), with 1440px as the reference viewport for every fluid value. Component spacing (`--s-xs` through `--s-2xl`) and section padding (`--sec-sm` through `--sec-xl`) are both `max()`-clamped between a fixed floor and a vw-scaled ceiling, so the page compresses gracefully at small widths without a breakpoint table. Beyond that shared rhythm, responsive behavior is handled case by case with Tailwind's `sm:`/`lg:` utilities — e.g. the Work row's 5-column layout collapses to a stacked mobile card, and the side-card stack (contact + "this is me") is desktop-only (`lg:` and up).

## Elevation & Depth

Flat, with two narrow exceptions — no `box-shadow` appears anywhere in `index.css`. Depth comes from (1) frosted floating chrome: the navbar and the right-hand side-card stack sit on `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`, always resolving to `rgb(0 0 0 / 0.92)` or its off-white inverse regardless of the live section theme; and (2) a fixed dark scrim (`rgb(0 0 0 / 0.85)`) behind the full-screen CaseStudy overlay.

### Named Rules
**The Blur-Is-Chrome Rule.** `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` is reserved to the navbar and the side-card stack — the two floating, always-visible pieces of chrome. It never appears on a card, tile, or in-flow element.

## Shapes

Radius reads as one family at three related steps, not a flat single value: 4px on primary chrome (the nav bar and side-cards, buttons, the contact card, work-strip tiles, spec tiles), stepping down to 3px on the avatar nested inside the contact card, and 2px on chips — each a shade tighter to read as inset within its 4px parent. Everything else is a hard, unrounded rectangle: sections, the grid, images inside their tiles, hairlines, the case-study overlay. Hairlines throughout — `.hairline`, work-row borders, the spec-table `<dl>` dividers, the button border — resolve to `rgb(ink / 0.2)`, one alpha step off whichever ink the live theme owns, so structural rules never need a per-theme color of their own. Element resets (`img`, `button`, `a`, heading/list margins) live in `@layer base`; unlayered they outranked Tailwind utilities and silently voided margins on `<p>`. The only fully circular forms are the live-clock dot and the language-switch's sliding knob.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Shape:** 4px radius, 1px border at rest in `--ink-50` (not the `--ink-20` hairline: a button boundary needs 3:1 non-text contrast on every theme, and the hairline measures 1.6–1.8:1).
- **Outline (`.btn`, default):** transparent fill, `1.125rem 1.25rem` padding, Label type. On hover a fill sweeps up from the bottom edge (`scaleY` on a pseudo-element, 450ms ease-out) and text/border invert to the theme ink; a trailing `↗` nudges 2px up-right (350ms).
- **Solid (`.btn--solid`):** pre-filled with the theme ink at rest — the inverse of Outline's hover state. Used for the kennismaking CTA ("Plan een kennismaking") in the hero, the case spread and Contact.
- **Focus:** `:focus-visible` gets a 2px solid outline in the current ink, offset 3px — the one focus treatment for every interactive element site-wide.

### Chips
- **Style:** a fixed black block (`rgb(0 0 0)`) with off-white Label-type text, 2px radius, no border; inverts to off-black-on-off-white only under `light`/`green`/`pink` theme selectors.
- **Groep chip (`.chip--groep`):** in the case spread only, the first chip is outlined instead of filled (transparent, `inset 0 0 0 1px var(--ink-50)`), so groep reads as a different kind of fact from herkomst and status. Work rows no longer carry it; the group header above them says it.
- **Use:** work-row metadata (`herkomst` / `status`) and case-study metadata (`groep` / `herkomst` / `status`) — never year or client, which live in the credits list.

### Cards / Containers
- **Contact card (`.card`):** black pill, 4px radius, a 40×40 avatar slot (3px radius) holding the N-mark; hovers by inverting to off-white-on-off-black (350ms).
- **Spec tile (`SpecTile`):** a black tile carrying a bracketed mono label over a value line — the stand-in plate for werkstukken with no photography yet (imagery still owed). Strip tiles are 4:3 (the first 16:10), the value is clamped to three lines with `hyphens: auto`, and every value in content stays short enough to fit without the clamp firing.
- **Case-study spread:** a full-viewport `.theme-dark` overlay over a `rgb(0 0 0 / 0.85)` scrim, with a sticky 2px progress hairline that fills as the visitor scrolls the spread; no border, no shadow beyond the scrim. Fixed chrome: "↖ Terug naar werk" top-left; top-right a "Kennismaking" contact card carrying the N-mark (contact stays one click away inside the spread and the mark identifies the site on a cold `/work/:slug` landing) next to the ✕; under 400px that card shows the N-mark only (label `sr-only`) so it never runs into "↖ Terug naar werk" at 320px / 400% zoom. The primary action on every spread is the solid "Plan een kennismaking" (`kennismakingLink`: booking URL when set, else a mailto with subject); live werkstukken add "Bezoek de site" as an outline second. A diagram-werkstuk shows the Diagram plate. Closing returns focus to the row that opened it.

### Oplossing Row (signature)
`Oplossingen.tsx`, a ruled `.row` list in the light section. Per row, 12 columns: the visitor's own sentence in second person at `.t-h3` (cols 1–5, 16ch, balanced; not a quote, there are no testimonials), the aanpak at `.t-body-md` (cols 6–9, 40ch), and a **proof tile** (`.proof`, cols 10–12): fixed black, 4px radius, 1px `--ink-20` border, a bracketed mono "Bewijs" label at 0.6 opacity, the werkstuk title at `.t-h6`, one scope-fact at `.t-body-sm`, and a typeset `( + )` bottom right. It is a real link to `/work/:slug` that opens the case spread in place via history (same `opensInPlace()` rule as the Work row: modified clicks stay native); on hover it inverts to off-black-on-off-white (350ms) and the `( + )` shifts 4px left.

### Werkwijze (signature)
`Werkwijze.tsx`, light section: a sticky (`lg:top-28`) Headline-size title in cols 1–5 beside a ruled `<dl>` in cols 6–12. Each guarantee is a hairline-topped row: a bracketed mono label (0.6 opacity, 10rem column), the kop at `.t-body-lg` (30ch, balanced) and the text at `.t-body` (52ch, 0.75 opacity).

### Diagram (signature)
`Diagram.tsx`, content-driven (replaces the retired McpDiagram/trace): a black tile with a header rule holding the kop and a bracketed `[ DIAGRAM ]` label, then an ordered list of steps, each a ruled row of mono label, `.t-h6` title and `.t-mono-md` note, joined by a typeset `↓` (the first row carries `·`); a mono-sm caption under the tile. Real text, read as an ordered list.

### Hero composition
**"Ruis → signaal" (9 Oct 2026).** The field opens as flickering static; a scan front with a bright scan line runs top → bottom (~2.1s, `uReveal`) and resolves it into the portrait while a mono readout beside the eyebrow counts `[ SIGNAAL 000% → 100% ]`. On the same row as the eyebrow and readout sits a typeset `( ❚❚ )` pause control (`aria-pressed`) that stops the video and the field (WCAG 2.2.2). Scrolling away scrubs the two headline lines apart (±14% x) while the field coarsens and dims (`uExit`). Below 640px the copy starts at 32svh so the head has room above it.
Eyebrow (`Mono bracket`), the full-width two-line `.t-display--hero` promise, then below it, in the left 6 columns: the practice line (`.t-body-lg`, 36ch) and the solid "Plan een kennismaking" CTA (`btn btn--solid`). Nothing sits in the right-hand columns; the portrait owns them. The BottomBar is the last row of the hero's flex column. The scrim is `.hero-text::before`, sized from the copy block (9rem above, 7rem below, full-bleed past the gutter, dark gradient 0.94 → 0), not a fixed share of the viewport.

### Navigation
- A single floating pill (a `<header>` landmark), `min(438px, 100%)` wide, centered at the top: the N-mark left (an `<a href="#hero">` home control), the active section's tagline center (Label type, re-decodes character-by-character on every section change), a three-line menu trigger right. The menu drops down **inside the pill** (grid-rows 0fr→1fr, 550ms): 50px rows in `.t-h6` with hairlines that draw from the left (staggered 45ms), a dotted pixel glyph at the right of each row, then the e-mail address with the NL/EN switch (first, so a phone reader finds the language switch without scrolling), an outline LinkedIn button and two solid CTAs ("Plan een gesprek" / "Start een project", both mailto with a subject). The page behind blurs and dims (`.menu-scrim`: `blur(16px)` + `rgb(0 0 0 / 0.4)`); the burger collapses to a single line. Esc, click-outside and any link close it. (6 Sep 2026 — replaces the full-screen overlay.) Menu items are real in-page links (`<a href="#oplossingen">` etc.): choosing one closes the menu, moves keyboard focus to the target section (`tabindex="-1"`, `focus({ preventScroll: true })`, no outline on `section[tabindex="-1"]:focus`) and Lenis scrolls there, so the next Tab continues from the section. The dropdown `<nav>` is `inert` while closed; Esc from inside it returns focus to the menu toggle.
- **Side stack** (desktop, `lg:` and up): a second floating column (an `<aside>` landmark), top-right, 160px wide — an off-white "Contact" card (mailto), then a "this is me" fold-out that grid-row-animates open (500ms ease) to an intro line, live availability status, and a LinkedIn link; the panel is `inert` while collapsed so its LinkedIn link is not an invisible tab stop. It stays above the menu scrim. (With the About details card, this panel is the only place availability shows.)

### Cursor (signature, lamalama, 9 Oct 2026)
Two layers, both off on touch (`pointer: coarse`); the native cursor stays.
- **Halftone wake** (`CursorTrail.tsx`): the pointer stirs a velocity field (gaussian, radius 4% of viewport height, 0.04 × travel in px) that decays every frame (`× (1 − min(0.5, dt/250))`). Wherever the field moves, the fixed 16px screen grid lights 2px dots on a 4px sub-grid in ordered-dither order, up to 7 of 16 per cell, densest where the pointer moved fastest. Dots take the live theme's ink (`--ink-rgb`, re-read on `data-theme` change). ### Text Motion — Reveal & Mono (signature)
`Reveal` masks every headline and lede: each word (or, for multi-line headlines, each line) sits in an `overflow: hidden` box and slides up from `yPercent: 110`, GSAP `power4.out`, 1.0s duration, staggered 0.014s per word or 0.09s per line, triggered once at 88% viewport entry. `Mono` decodes labels into place on entry: characters cycle through a glyph pool (`01<>/[]{}#%&*+=-_$@`) and settle left to right over 600–700ms; the scrambling span is `aria-hidden` and an `sr-only` twin carries the final text, so assistive tech never reads the glyph soup. `.rv > span` carries no standing `will-change: transform` (it permanently promoted 162 layers); GSAP promotes each word only while it moves. Both are inert under `prefers-reduced-motion` — Reveal renders in place, Mono prints the final text immediately.

### Dither Field (signature)
**Source and placement (9 Oct 2026).** The field reads `public/hero-signal.mp4` / `hero-signal.jpg`: the same 13s loop, cut out per frame (u2net human-seg mask, temporally smoothed) and graded for the field — subject CLAHE-lifted with a floor so hair and shirt keep dot texture, fog held at ≤0.34, a rim on the silhouette brightest on the lit side. The head is no longer placed by cover-fit: `zone()` measures where the copy starts and sets `uPlace` (screen spot + scale) so the chin stands just inside the text zone with open air above (20–42% of viewport height, 26% max on phones; the crown never above 95% of the height (90% on phones) so the nav keeps clear ground; on short screens it pins there and the chin runs behind the eyebrow), right of centre at x 0.76 (0.62 on phones); beyond the source the field dissolves to empty. **The portrait is a supporting voice (9 Oct 2026):** its ink is held at 0.62 in the glyph pass while all seven glyph levels stay, so the face keeps its structure and only gets quieter (the acquire static too; the scan line, flagged in the field's g channel, keeps full ink — a first try that scaled luminance instead collapsed the face to ~4 levels and lost the likeness) and the pointer never touches it — the lens is gone, because it warped the face. Any later hero portrait (the planned point cloud included) inherits both rules: subordinate to the headline, never deformed by the pointer. **Rest-ruis (9 Oct 2026):** the source is black around the head, so the smaller head left a mostly empty field. The carrier now stays after the lock: in the cell pass two octaves of value noise (scale 2.6 per viewport height, the two octaves drifting against each other at ~0.03–0.05/s so patches change shape as they travel) form patchy mist, and a per-cell hash, re-rolled every ~1.4s per cell on a staggered phase so the mist simmers, against that density turns on the two lowest glyphs (`.` and `:`, roughly 15–25% of cells inside a patch). It stays off the head (ellipse, radius ~0.14–0.32), thins by 70% inside the text-safe zone, and drifts only while video frames arrive, so the pause control stops it too. Beyond ~0.4 from the head the source's own fog is faded out, because the frame edge cut it into a hard block; the rest-ruis owns that ground. Seven glyph levels (`. : + o ‡ █`), an unsharp contour at two cells' reach, and brightness that rises with the level. The original `hero.mp4` / `hero.jpg` stay in `public/` for rollback only.

The hero's only full-bleed surface: an OGL WebGL shader (`Dither.tsx`) quantizing the luminance of a looping `<video>` (`public/hero-signal.mp4`, poster `hero-signal.jpg`) into a grid of 3×5 monospace-glyph bitmaps at 6px cells (5px under 640px). The source is Nils himself — his own photos restaged by Higgsfield into a monochrome chiaroscuro profile (right of centre, eyes open, facing the headline) in drifting studio fog, so the "Eén persoon" headline and the figure tell the same true story. The field ignores the pointer entirely (no trail, no `uTrail`, no lens; the site-wide halftone wake lives in CursorTrail). Behind the copy the field holds a **text-safe zone** (`uSafe`, measured from the hero's text grid and re-measured on resize, font load and the NL/EN switch): from 16px above the copy down, glyph levels drop by 45% and the ink is switched off, fading in over ~96px above. At rest the brightest glyph pixel stays ≥4.6:1 against off-white anyway; the zone exists so the 11px eyebrow and headline sit on calmer texture. Any later hero material inherits the same rule. The loop is a 15s Wan clip closed with a 2s ffmpeg `xfade` (last 2s dissolve into the first 2s), so the fog never reverses or snaps; the seam differs from its neighbours no more than any other frame step. Cost is two passes: a cell pass (one fragment per 6px cell: placement, texture fetch, contour) into a tiny render target, then a glyph pass that only stamps the 3×5 bitmap at full resolution — no noise in the shader (21 Sep 2026: the fbm field, worms and colour-drift fbm were dropped after the old shader pegged the GPU; 22 Sep 2026: the per-cell math moved off the per-fragment path). It renders at 30fps, skips frames when no video frame arrived, uploads a frame via `requestVideoFrameCallback` (see failure path), pauses the video and the loop once off-screen via `IntersectionObserver`, and never starts the video under `prefers-reduced-motion` (the poster stays). iOS Low Power Mode refuses autoplay and falls back to the poster the same way. **Failure path:** if WebGL cannot be created (Lockdown Mode, blocklisted GPU, some remote desktops) or the field throws, a `FieldBoundary` error boundary / try-catch leaves the hero on its dark ground with the copy intact and settles the readout at 100%. Where `requestVideoFrameCallback` is missing, the field re-uploads on its own 30fps tick while the video plays.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep `data-theme` section-owned — set only from `watchSections()`'s 50%-viewport trigger, never from a user control.
- **Do** keep red to the N-mark and the clock dot; keep green to the Contact section's field.
- **Do** typeset brackets, parens, and arrows as characters (`.bracket`, `.paren`, `↗`), never as icons or borders.
- **Do** round primary chrome to 4px, step avatars to 3px and chips to 2px, and leave everything else square.
- **Do** run every text entrance through `Reveal`'s word/line mask and respect `prefers-reduced-motion` everywhere motion appears.

### Don't:
- **Don't** add a manual theme toggle; the section-owned model is a settled product decision (6 Sep 2026), not a placeholder.
- **Don't** introduce a second WebGL surface outside the hero — the dither field is deliberately singular.
- **Don't** use `box-shadow` for decoration; the system's only depth cues are backdrop blur on floating chrome and the modal scrim.
- **Don't** reintroduce the retired "Concrete Monograph" vocabulary (Archivo Expanded, Source Serif, oxide accent, radius 0 everywhere) — it is an anti-reference, not a fallback.
- **Don't** wire the reserved `pink`/`blue` theme grounds to a section without an explicit decision; they exist in `index.css` but are inert.
