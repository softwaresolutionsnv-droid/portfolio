# Design System: Nils Vogelaar — Portfolio

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Signal"**

The portfolio is a literal translation of lamalama.com's grammar (pinned by the owner, 6 Sep 2026) into a solo practice's proof-site: bold grotesk caps at line-height .82, a mono UI voice typeset in `[ brackets ]` and `( parens )`, a full-bleed dither field as the hero's one material, a floating nav with a per-section tagline, a right-hand card stack, work as a ruled list with image strips, and themes owned entirely by the section in view. It replaces "Concrete Monograph" (Archivo Expanded + serif, oxide accent, radius 0) outright — nothing from that world survives, and it is now an anti-reference, not a fallback.

Scrolling is Lenis-smoothed (`lerp: 0.1`) and drives every section boundary: `watchSections()` (`src/lib/motion.ts`) flips `data-theme` on `<html>` the moment a section crosses 50% of the viewport, in either scroll direction. There is no visitor-facing toggle — the theme is a property of what is currently on screen, not a preference.

Two moments carry the display type at full scale (the hero headline, the Contact title); everywhere else — nav, buttons, chips, meta, captions — speaks in Sometype Mono. Color is just as rationed: one dark ground, one off-white ground, one green field, and a single red accent confined to two literal marks.

**Key Characteristics:**
- One dark ground, one off-white ground, one green field — section-owned, never chosen by the visitor
- Geist bold caps at line-height 0.82 for the two display moments; Sometype Mono is the UI voice everywhere else
- 4px radius on primary chrome (nav, cards, buttons, tiles), stepping to 3px on avatars and 2px on chips; nothing else rounds
- Signal red is reserved to exactly two marks: the N-mark's third bar and the live-clock dot
- A glyph-dither shader is the hero's one WebGL surface and stays there
- Every text entrance is a GSAP word/line mask; every motion effect disables under `prefers-reduced-motion`

## Colors

A duotone chrome lit by one signal accent and one section-owned field; two further hues are wired into the theme system but not yet assigned to any section.

### Primary
- **Signal Red** (`rgb(230 57 70)`): the system's one true accent. Used in exactly two places in the whole codebase — the N-mark's third bar (`Mark.tsx`) and the live-clock dot's blink (`BottomBar.tsx`).

### Secondary
- **Signal Green** (`rgb(208 255 126)`): the Contact section's field, entered only via `[data-theme="green"]` — the page's one non-monochrome ground, and its closing note.

### Neutral
- **Dark** (`rgb(26 28 28)`): the hero/work ground; also the ink used on the off-white and green sections.
- **Black** (`rgb(0 0 0)`): the fixed background of chrome that ignores section theme entirely — chips, the contact card, spec tiles, the menu overlay.
- **White** (`rgb(255 255 255)`): the elevated tone on light/green sections (`--bg2`), mirroring Black's role on dark.
- **Off-Black** (`rgb(12 12 12)`): the ink on light/green sections.
- **Off-White** (`rgb(249 244 235)`): the services/about ground; also the ink used on the dark section.

### Reserved (unused)
- **Pink** (`rgb(255 198 218)`) and **Blue** (`rgb(220 242 244)`): a fourth `data-theme="pink"` ground and a blue swatch are fully defined in `index.css` (chip, navbar and side-card selectors already branch on `pink`), but `App.tsx`'s section list only ever assigns `dark`, `light`, or `green`. Both are inert until a section claims them.

### Named Rules
**The Section-Owned Theme Rule.** `data-theme` is set only by `watchSections()`'s 50%-viewport trigger — never by a stored preference or a visible control.
**The One Signal Rule.** Red appears nowhere but the N-mark and the clock dot. It is not a hover, link, or focus color.
**The Fixed-Black Rule.** Chips, the contact card, spec tiles, and the menu overlay hold a black/off-white pairing at all times — they read against `rgb(0 0 0)` regardless of the live section theme, not against the theme's own `--bg2`.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Geist (variable, loaded 300–700; the live scale only calls on 400 and 700).
**UI / Label Font:** Sometype Mono (400, 500) — every piece of interface voice: nav, labels, chips, buttons, meta bar, captions.

**Character:** a grotesk display set tight enough to look poured, against a mono voice that never exceeds 0.75rem — one confident headline moment surrounded by a quiet, technical register.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (700, `clamp(3.25rem, 1.0625rem + 4.38vw, 7.7rem)`, lh 0.82, uppercase, tracking -0.02em): `.t-display` — the hero headline and the Contact section title. The system's only two full-scale display settings.
- **Headline** (700, `clamp(3rem, 1.125rem + 3.75vw, 6.75rem)`, lh 0.82, uppercase): `.t-display-sm` — the Services title, the case-study title, and the menu overlay's link list.
- **Title** (400, `clamp(1.5rem, 0.875rem + 1.25vw, 2.66rem)`, lh 1.05): `.t-h4` — work-row titles and the case-study lede.
- **Body Large** (400, `clamp(1.25rem, 0.9375rem + 0.63vw, 1.8rem)`, lh 1.2): the hero practice line, the Contact pitch.
- **Body** (400, `clamp(0.9375rem, 0.85rem + 0.2vw, 1.14rem)`, lh 1.4): running copy — Services list items, credit values, spec-tile text.
- **Label** (400, 0.6875rem, lh 1.6, uppercase): `.t-mono` — the site's UI voice: nav tagline, buttons, chips, meta bar, every `Mono` component label.

Two intermediate display sizes exist outside the frontmatter roles: `.t-h2` (`clamp(2rem, 0.75rem + 2.5vw, 4.5rem)`, sentence case) for the Work section's intro line, and `.t-h3` (`clamp(1.75rem, 0.8125rem + 1.88vw, 3.5rem)`) for the About section's drop-indented intro paragraph. Two smaller mono steps, `.t-mono-sm` (0.625rem, weight 500) and `.t-mono-md` (0.75rem), carry chip and spec-tile captions; `.t-code` (0.8125rem monospace) carries the garmin-mcp trace block.

### Named Rules
**The Bracket Rule.** Mono labels are typeset, not bordered: `.bracket` wraps content in a literal `[ ` … ` ]`, `.paren` in `( ` … ` )` via CSS `content`. Arrows (`↗ ↖ ← →`) are typeset characters, never icon glyphs.
**The Tight-Cap Rule.** Every uppercase display setting (Display, Headline) sits at line-height 0.82 with -0.02em tracking; that combination never appears at body or label sizes.

## Layout

A 12-column grid (`.grid-12`, column gap `var(--gap)`) inside a fluid-margin container (`.container`, side padding `max(1rem, 40/1440 · 100vw)`), with 1440px as the reference viewport for every fluid value. Component spacing (`--s-xs` through `--s-2xl`) and section padding (`--sec-sm` through `--sec-xl`) are both `max()`-clamped between a fixed floor and a vw-scaled ceiling, so the page compresses gracefully at small widths without a breakpoint table. Beyond that shared rhythm, responsive behavior is handled case by case with Tailwind's `sm:`/`lg:` utilities — e.g. the Work row's 5-column layout collapses to a stacked mobile card, and the side-card stack (contact + "this is me") is desktop-only (`lg:` and up).

## Elevation & Depth

Flat, with two narrow exceptions — no `box-shadow` appears anywhere in `index.css`. Depth comes from (1) frosted floating chrome: the navbar and the right-hand side-card stack sit on `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`, always resolving to `rgb(0 0 0 / 0.92)` or its off-white inverse regardless of the live section theme; and (2) a fixed dark scrim (`rgb(0 0 0 / 0.85)`) behind the full-screen CaseStudy overlay.

### Named Rules
**The Blur-Is-Chrome Rule.** `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` is reserved to the navbar and the side-card stack — the two floating, always-visible pieces of chrome. It never appears on a card, tile, or in-flow element.

## Shapes

Radius reads as one family at three related steps, not a flat single value: 4px on primary chrome (the nav bar and side-cards, buttons, the contact card, work-strip tiles, spec tiles), stepping down to 3px on the avatar nested inside the contact card, and 2px on chips — each a shade tighter to read as inset within its 4px parent. Everything else is a hard, unrounded rectangle: sections, the grid, images inside their tiles, hairlines, the case-study overlay. Hairlines throughout — `.hairline`, work-row borders, the spec-table `<dl>` dividers, the button border — resolve to `rgb(ink / 0.2)`, one alpha step off whichever ink the live theme owns, so structural rules never need a per-theme color of their own. The only fully circular forms are the live-clock dot and the language-switch's sliding knob.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Shape:** 4px radius, 1px hairline border at rest.
- **Outline (`.btn`, default):** transparent fill, `1.125rem 1.25rem` padding, Label type. On hover a fill sweeps up from the bottom edge (`scaleY` on a pseudo-element, 450ms ease-out) and text/border invert to the theme ink; a trailing `↗` nudges 2px up-right (350ms).
- **Solid (`.btn--solid`):** pre-filled with the theme ink at rest — the inverse of Outline's hover state. Used for the case study's primary CTA.
- **Focus:** `:focus-visible` gets a 2px solid outline in the current ink, offset 3px — the one focus treatment for every interactive element site-wide.

### Chips
- **Style:** a fixed black block (`rgb(0 0 0)`) with off-white Label-type text, 2px radius, no border; inverts to off-black-on-off-white only under `light`/`green`/`pink` theme selectors.
- **Use:** work-row and case-study metadata (`groep` / `herkomst` / `status`).

### Cards / Containers
- **Contact card (`.card`):** black pill, 4px radius, a 40×40 avatar slot (3px radius) holding the N-mark; hovers by inverting to off-white-on-off-black (350ms).
- **Spec tile (`SpecTile`):** a black tile carrying a bracketed mono label over a value line — the stand-in plate for werkstukken with no photography yet (garmin-mcp's trace, or imagery still owed).
- **Case-study spread:** a full-viewport `.theme-dark` overlay over a `rgb(0 0 0 / 0.85)` scrim, with a sticky 2px progress hairline that fills as the visitor scrolls the spread; no border, no shadow beyond the scrim.

### Navigation
- A single floating pill, `min(438px, 100%)` wide, centered at the top: the N-mark left (returns to `#hero`), the active section's tagline center (Label type, re-decodes character-by-character on every section change), a three-line menu trigger right. The menu drops down **inside the pill** (grid-rows 0fr→1fr, 550ms): 50px rows in `.t-h6` with hairlines that draw from the left (staggered 45ms), a dotted pixel glyph at the right of each row, then an outline LinkedIn button and two solid CTAs ("Plan een gesprek" / "Start een project", both mailto with a subject), the e-mail address and the NL/EN switch. The page behind blurs and dims (`.menu-scrim`: `blur(16px)` + `rgb(0 0 0 / 0.4)`); the burger collapses to a single line. Esc, click-outside and any link close it. (6 Sep 2026 — replaces the full-screen overlay.)
- **Side stack** (desktop, `lg:` and up): a second floating column, top-right, 160px wide — an off-white "Contact" card (mailto), then a "this is me" fold-out that grid-row-animates open (500ms ease) to an intro line, live availability status, and a LinkedIn link. It stays above the menu scrim.

### Cursor label (signature)
`CursorLabel.tsx`: one fixed mono tag (48px tall, `rgb(255 255 255 / 0.14)` + `blur(10px)`, 4px radius) that trails the pointer with GSAP `quickTo` (350ms `power3.out`) and shows the `data-cursor` text of whatever it is over — today the work rows (`"<title> ( + )"`). It hides on scroll, on pointer-leave, on touch (`pointer: coarse`) and under reduced motion.

### Work Row (signature)
A ruled list row (`.row`, 1px top hairline, last row also closes with one), 12 columns: title (`.t-h6`, cols 1–2) · metadata chips (cols 3–5, each decoding in via `Mono`) · a `( + )` mono affordance (col 6) that reads `( BEKIJK + )` on hover · an image strip from col 7 that runs past the right edge of the viewport (tiles 120px tall at 1440, 8px gap, 4px radius, natural aspect; mobile captures `-m-NN` are portrait 390/844). On hover the tiles scale 1.04 (900ms) and the strip shifts left 1.5rem (700ms). Rows fade-slide in (`y` 28→0, 900ms `power4.out`) as they enter at 92%. Below `lg` the strip is an in-flow horizontal scroller.

### BottomBar (signature)
One ruled meta bar for the whole page, `position: sticky; bottom: 0` as the last child of `<main>`: it floats at the viewport bottom over every section (`rgb(bg / 0.86)` + `blur(12px)`, hairline drawing in from the left on load) and docks in flow under the Contact section's copyright line. Below 768px it is static in the footer only. Items: one hairline, then Label-type items — role, city, an Amsterdam wall clock (`[ ● HH : MM : SS ]`, ticking every second, tabular figures, the dot blinking on a 1.6s cycle), social links suffixed with a typeset `+`, and the NL/EN switch (a 22×10px pill with a sliding 6px dot, 300ms).

### Text Motion — Reveal & Mono (signature)
`Reveal` masks every headline and lede: each word (or, for multi-line headlines, each line) sits in an `overflow: hidden` box and slides up from `yPercent: 110`, GSAP `power4.out`, 1.0s duration, staggered 0.014s per word or 0.09s per line, triggered once at 88% viewport entry. `Mono` decodes labels into place on entry: characters cycle through a glyph pool (`01<>/[]{}#%&*+=-_$@`) and settle left to right over 600–700ms. Both are inert under `prefers-reduced-motion` — Reveal renders in place, Mono prints the final text immediately.

### Dither Field (signature)
The hero's only full-bleed surface: an OGL WebGL shader (`Dither.tsx`) quantizing a drifting noise field (or a `<video>` source, wired but unused) into a grid of 3×5 monospace-glyph bitmaps at 6px cells (5px under 640px). **Organic worms** — contour bands of a domain-warped fbm field (`fract(w·2.5)` bands, 0.03–0.05 wide) — are punched out of the glyph field as near-black labyrinth shapes, the literal counterpart of lamalama's halftone contour. The pointer and a 12-sample trail (`uTrail`) push the worms open and light the field (`ink`), so moving the mouse smears a soft glow through the hero; on touch the pointer starts off-field and nothing lights up. A mouse-follow lens distortion remains. It freezes on its first frame under `prefers-reduced-motion`, and pauses entirely once off-screen via `IntersectionObserver`.

## Recorded exceptions

- **The `[ … ]` eyebrow above a display heading is deliberate.** The craft floor bans kickers; the owner's pinned reference (lamalama.com) uses a bracketed mono label above every headline, and the literal-translation brief keeps it (decision of 6 Sep 2026). It is a mono `Mono bracket` label, never a styled kicker.
- **The first-line indent on the about lede (`text-indent: 2.6em`)** mirrors the reference's culture block, where the opening line is indented against a flush-left paragraph. It appears only there.
- **Chip radius is 2px** where other chrome is 4px, matching the reference's tag blocks.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep `data-theme` section-owned — set only from `watchSections()`'s 50%-viewport trigger, never from a user control.
- **Do** keep red to the N-mark and the clock dot; keep green to the Contact section's field.
- **Do** typeset brackets, parens, and arrows as characters (`.bracket`, `.paren`, `↗`), never as icons or borders.
- **Do** round primary chrome to 4px, step avatars to 3px and chips to 2px, and leave everything else square.
- **Do** run every text entrance through `Reveal`'s word/line mask and respect `prefers-reduced-motion` everywhere motion appears.

### Don't:
- **Don't** add a manual theme toggle; the section-owned model is a settled product decision (6 Sep 2026), not a placeholder.
- **Don't** introduce a second WebGL surface outside the hero — the dither field is deliberately singular.
- **Don't** use `box-shadow` for decoration; the system's only depth cues are backdrop blur on floating chrome and the modal scrim.
- **Don't** reintroduce the retired "Concrete Monograph" vocabulary (Archivo Expanded, Source Serif, oxide accent, radius 0 everywhere) — it is an anti-reference, not a fallback.
- **Don't** wire the reserved `pink`/`blue` theme grounds to a section without an explicit decision; they exist in `index.css` but are inert.
